## Material Symbols

The `sym_*.xml` vector drawables in `app/src/main/res/drawable` are adapted from Google's Material Symbols Android assets. The paths are from the Material Symbols Outlined set, except `sym_dark_mode.xml`, which uses the Material Symbols dark mode glyph. Google's symbols are licensed under Apache License 2.0.

- Source: https://github.com/google/material-design-icons/tree/master/symbols/android
- License: https://github.com/google/material-design-icons/blob/master/LICENSE

The assets have been renamed for this project and their XML tint attributes were removed so Compose Material 3 can apply the current content color.
